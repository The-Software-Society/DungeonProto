# Emberfall Update — Master Plan (main quest line + world overhaul)

Big autonomous build (started 2026-07-11). Source of truth for the whole effort; update the Status column as things land. Everything is named **Emberfall**: Emberfall (town), Emberfall Forest (start), Emberfall Dungeon (first dungeon).

## Vision (from Jon)
A first-login main quest line: meet **Aldric** (old, weathered, quietly powerful mentor — Master-Roshi-serious, world-worn hermit hiding in the forest) in a cutscene; he gives your first weapon (player CHOICE: sword+focus OR a main magic weapon) and teaches skills, fishing, eating. Forest becomes big + real (Unity Terrain, GPU wind grass, real trees/dirt, a river fishing area with the twerking goblins). The "weird middle" becomes a real town (vendor, gear-upgrader, rescue-quest giver). Grind gear, combine +1+ +1 → +2..+5, sell/spend. Mini-boss variants of ALL mobs drop rare gear. Dungeon becomes bigger/realistic with locked rooms (no mage-cheese) + a boss, dungeon-interior mobs don't respawn. Rescue quest (town): free a kidnapped daughter from **Grukmar** the orc warlord. Party system (invite, member UI, full shared XP + shared quest/kill/rescue credit). Loot: yours 60s (party sees it if grouped) then public. Duel: right-click → request → accept → only those two damage each other → loser drops to 1 HP, never dies, both reset. Vistas (GuildWars-style): forest tree-climb puzzle + a town vista, valuable summit rewards. Items: real per-item icons, +X badge, tooltip shows hovered item AND your equipped counterpart. Cutscenes for all quests, on-screen text only. Main quest eventually leads to clearing the main dungeon.
