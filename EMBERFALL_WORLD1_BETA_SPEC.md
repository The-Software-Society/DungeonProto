# Emberfall World 1 Beta Spec

Updated: 2026-07-12

This document is the concrete planning spec for World 1 beta. It defines the quests, NPCs, skills,
first-clear rewards, mobs, drops, and launch planning assumptions for Emberfall.

## Scope

Game: **Aldenfall**

World 1: **Emberfall**

World 1 contains:

- Emberfall City
- Emberfall Forest
- Emberfall Lake
- Emberfall Deepwood
- Emberfall Warcamp
- Emberfall Volcano
- Emberfall Dungeon

Players begin knowing nothing about other worlds. World 1 starts as a local fantasy mystery: death is
not behaving correctly. The ending reveals that Emberfall was only the first world.

## Opening Cutscene

The player starts with a bespoke cinematic, not a plain spawn.

Tone reference:

- Kingdom Hearts-style mysterious arrival.
- Isekai-like materialization into an unfamiliar world.
- Beautiful, disorienting, and lightly ominous rather than exposition-heavy.

Sequence:

1. Brief normal-life scene before Aldenfall: ordinary environment, music, motion, no huge playable
   world required.
2. The player dies in a striking but not over-explained moment.
3. Darkness, distant sound, particles, fragments of sky/stone/embers.
4. The player particlizes into Aldric's forest glade.
5. Aldric is present and visibly surprised.
6. He studies the player, realizes they know nothing, and shifts into practical survival mode.
7. The player sees Emberfall's volcano in the distance, dormant and quiet.

Opening intent:

- The player did not simply "wake up" as a local.
- The player had a normal life before arriving and died before materializing in Emberfall.
- Aldric did not casually summon them.
- The arrival is strange and important, but not explained yet.
- Aldric becomes the player's first anchor in a world they do not understand.

## Beta Time Targets

- Main story path: 5-10 hours.
- Main story plus meaningful side content: 10-20 hours.
- Completionist World 1: 20+ hours.
- Grinder: indefinite, with diminishing relevance across future worlds.

Normal first-time boss-ready target:

- Main combat skill around 15-30.
- Defence around 10-25 depending on playstyle.
- Fishing/Cooking around 5-15 if following story and preparing.
- Grinders may push far higher, including 99+ as a discovery moment.

## World 1 Main Story

### Act 1: Nobody Knows Anything

The player particlizes into Aldric's glade in front of him, learns survival, and sees small hints
that death is wrong.

### Act 2: Emberfall Matters

The player meets Emberfall City, learns its economy and people, and becomes invested before the
volcano crisis.

### Act 3: Local Threats

The player handles goblins, bandits, orcs, and Grukmar. The daughter rescue leads into an orc attack
on the city.

### Act 4: Death Proof

During the orc attack, an NPC dies and returns. The city realizes this is not rumor. Aldric reacts as
if he has feared this moment.

### Act 5: Volcano Mystery

The volcano was known and tested as dormant. Something or someone caused it to erupt and left a
guardian inside to protect the source.

### Act 6: First Clear

The first team clears Emberfall Dungeon and stabilizes the world.

### Act 7: Dragon Hook

After the dungeon clear, a dragon bursts through a breach, flies over Emberfall City, and escapes
back through the breach into another world. The dragon is teased as World 2's boss, not fought in
World 1.

### Act 8: Beyond Emberfall

After the dragon breach proves that other worlds exist, Aldric volunteers to help lead the mission
into World 2 alongside the player, the outsider who cleared Emberfall Dungeon and saved the city. He
does not reveal the true method of world travel yet. That reveal should happen after several World 2
mainline quests, once the party has reached World 2's main city and Aldric admits he can no longer
hide what the player needs.

## Main Line Quests

### 1. A Word from Aldric

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Experience a short normal-life opening scene.
- Die before entering Aldenfall.
- Particlize into Aldric's glade during the opening cutscene.
- Speak with Aldric.
- Learn basic interaction.

Completion:

- Dialogue completes.

Rewards:

- 10 gold.
- Unlocks starter training.
- First Aldric relationship flag.

Story:

- Aldric is introduced as a homeless, sharp old man with more awareness than he admits. He is
  surprised by the player's arrival but quickly becomes practical.

Cutscene:

- Opening Kingdom Hearts-like sequence: normal life, sudden death, particles, disorientation, Aldric
  by fire, the player forming in front of him, distant Emberfall volcano visible but quiet.

### 2. Choose Your Edge

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Choose melee starter or magic starter.
- Learn that this is an opening preference, not a class lock.
- Inspect or equip the starter item.

Completion:

- Equip chosen weapon or confirm loadout.

Rewards:

- Melee: Rusty Sword.
- Magic: Apprentice Wand.
- Both paths should remain reversible later; this is not a class lock.

Story:

- Aldric explains that skill comes from use, not birthright. Anyone can train anything: pures can
  specialize in Magic, Strength, Archery, etc.; mains can level everything; gear is not bound by a
  class identity.

### 3. Find Your Footing

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Complete a sequence of small tutorial objectives, each treated as its own subquest/beat rather
  than one giant checklist:
  - Move with WASD.
  - Rotate camera / aim.
  - Open inventory.
  - Open quest log.
  - Open map.
  - Equip or inspect weapon.
  - Use a basic ability.
  - Learn why ability requirements matter.
  - Kill a weak monster.
  - Gain enough use/mastery XP to reach mastery rank 2 on the starter ability or equivalent early
    mastery milestone.

Completion:

- All required basic controls and first combat beats complete.

Rewards:

- 25 gold.
- Small XP pulse in used skill.
- Starter ability mastery progress.

Story:

- Teaches movement, UI, inventory, quests, map, equipment, ability requirements, combat, skill XP,
  and ability mastery through separate digestible beats.

### 4. First Blood, First Return

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Investigate a nearby dead traveler or guard marker.
- Witness a rumor that someone returned after death.

Completion:

- Inspect marker and report to Aldric.

Rewards:

- 15 gold.
- Lore entry: "Death Rumors."

Story:

- First light foreshadowing. This should not fully prove death is broken yet.

### 5. A Fisher's Patience

NPC: Aldric or Lakeside Fisher

Player does:

- Travel to Emberfall Lake.
- Catch any fish.

Completion:

- CatchFish objective.

Rewards:

- Fishing Pole if not already granted.
- 15 gold.
- Fishing XP.

Story:

- Teaches that non-combat skills matter.

### 6. Cook, Then Eat

NPC: Aldric or Lakeside Fisher

Player does:

- Cook a fish.
- Eat cooked food.

Completion:

- CookFood and EatFood objectives.

Rewards:

- 20 gold.
- 3 Cooked Fish.
- Cooking XP.

Story:

- Teaches preparation and survival.

### 7. First Steps in Emberfall

NPC: Aldric, then city NPCs

Player does:

- Enter Emberfall City.
- Visit the fountain.
- Speak with Marla the Trader.
- Visit the forge.

Completion:

- Reach city marker and talk to required NPCs.

Rewards:

- 40 gold.
- Unlocks basic vendor/forge tutorial.

Story:

- The city becomes a place with people and routines, not just a quest hub.

Cutscene:

- First reveal of Emberfall City: fountain, market, forge, citizens, gates.

### 8. A Mother's Plea

NPC: Elowen, Worried Mother

Player does:

- Speak with Elowen.
- Travel to Emberfall Warcamp.
- Defeat Grukmar.
- Free Elowen's daughter, Mira.
- Return to Elowen.

Completion:

- Grukmar kill.
- Captive interaction.
- Return dialogue.

Rewards:

- 150 gold.
- Reward choice when possible:
  - Knight's Sword for melee.
  - Adept Focus for magic.
- If reward choice UI is not implemented, grant one strong universal reward plus a later shop path.

Story:

- First major emotional quest. The player saves someone specific.

Cutscenes:

- Mother's plea.
- Mira freed.
- Mother/daughter reunion.

### 9. Smoke on the Roads

NPC: Marla or Captain Rusk

Player does:

- Clear goblins on the west road.
- Clear bandits on the east road.

Completion:

- Kill 4 Goblin Scrappers.
- Kill 4 Field Bandits.

Rewards:

- 120 gold.
- Iron Helm or Apprentice/Adept Focus equivalent.
- Unlocks repeatable bounty board.

Story:

- The rescue exposed larger instability. Roads are unsafe and trade is failing.

### 10. Retaliation at Emberfall Gate

NPC: Captain Rusk

Player does:

- Defend Emberfall City from an orc attack.
- Fight orcs at the gate/plaza.
- Witness an NPC death and respawn.

Completion:

- Defeat attack waves.
- Survive or complete event timer.
- Speak with Aldric afterward.

Rewards:

- 175 gold.
- City reputation flag.
- Unlocks "Death Broke" main mystery arc.

Story:

- The orcs retaliate after Grukmar's defeat. During the attack, a known guard or citizen dies and
  returns. This is the first undeniable proof that death is broken.

Cutscene:

- Guard dies defending gate.
- Moments later, same guard appears at the fountain or an old stone, confused.
- Aldric says something restrained, such as: "No. Not here. Not already."

### 11. Old Ash, New Fire

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Confront Aldric about the death event.
- Investigate old seal stones in Emberfall Forest or Deepwood.

Completion:

- Speak to Aldric.
- Inspect 2-3 seal marks.

Rewards:

- 80 gold.
- Lore entry: "Old Marks."

Story:

- Aldric admits the volcano should not be active and that death changing is tied to something older.
  He does not reveal his role.

### 12. Smoke on the Horizon

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Scout Emberfall Approach.
- Reach ash/ember zone.
- Find acolyte signs or a volcanic device/mark.

Completion:

- ReachLocation objective.
- Optional: defeat one Ember Acolyte.

Rewards:

- 100 gold.
- Dungeon warning.

Story:

- The dormant volcano is awakening. Something caused it.

Cutscene:

- The volcano glows; ash falls; the player sees lava and unnatural marks.

### 13. Ready for the Caldera

NPC: Aldric, Forge NPC, Marla

Player does:

- Complete any two preparation paths:
  - Upgrade an equipped item.
  - Cook 3 healing foods.
  - Defeat a dire enemy.
  - Reach a recommended combat level threshold.
  - Buy/sell with Marla.

Completion:

- Best implementation: alternate-objective quest support.
- Temporary implementation: require cook 3 foods plus upgrade or dire kill.

Rewards:

- Dungeon supply bundle:
  - Cooked Fish or better food.
  - Gold.
  - Optional one-time revive/teleport item later.

Story:

- Teaches that players can prepare through combat, economy, skills, or food.

### 14. The Heart of Emberfall

NPC: Aldric / Dungeon entrance trigger

Player does:

- Enter Emberfall Dungeon.
- Trigger eruption.
- Clear dungeon encounters.
- Defeat the Volcano Guardian.

Completion:

- Final boss kill.
- World state changes from Erupting to Cleared.

Rewards:

- 400 gold.
- Choice:
  - Emberfang for melee.
  - Ember Focus for magic.
- Personal World 1 clear flag.
- Eligibility for post-clear quest.

Story:

- The guardian protects whatever awakened the dormant volcano. It is not the mastermind.

Cutscenes:

- Dungeon entry/eruption.
- Boss intro.
- Boss defeat.

### 15. The Breach Beyond

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Walk beyond the boss chamber.
- Witness the dragon breach.
- Return to Emberfall City or Aldric.

Completion:

- Cutscene watched and epilogue dialogue completed.

Rewards:

- Title later: Emberfall Clearer.
- Unlocks World 2 teaser state.

Story:

- A dragon tears through an impossible breach, flies over Emberfall City, and returns through the
  breach. Players discover Emberfall was only the beginning.

Cutscene:

- Major beta ending scene.

### 16. Beyond Emberfall

NPC: Aldric

Player does:

- Speak with Aldric after the dragon breach.
- Hear Aldric volunteer to help lead a mission beyond Emberfall.
- Enter World 2 through the breach/aftermath route exposed by the dragon.
- Explore the early outskirts of World 2 with Aldric's guidance.
- Begin the World 2 mainline toward its main city.

Completion:

- Player reaches the first World 2 staging area or outskirts marker.
- Aldric joins/commits to the World 2 mission.

Rewards:

- World 2 prologue access flag.
- Minor gold or travel token.
- Mainline transition started.

Story:

- Aldric does not reveal the true transportation method yet. He volunteers because the player saved
  Emberfall, the dragon made waiting impossible, and the city needs someone to understand what lies
  beyond. Only after World 2 quests lead to the main city should Aldric reveal that he has been
  hiding a method the player will need.

Cutscene:

- Aldric approaches the breach, studies it, and chooses to come with the player. The scene ends with
  the first playable view of World 2's outskirts, not the full city reveal.

## Skills

### World 1 / Beta Skills

Recommended beta skills:

- Health / HP
- Attack
- Strength
- Defence
- Magic
- Archery
- Fishing
- Cooking
- Mining
- Smithing/Forging
- Woodcutting
- Fletching

Current implemented skills:

- Attack
- Strength
- Defence
- Magic
- Fishing
- Cooking

Recommendation:

- Health/HP should be part of the progression model. It can level through taking damage, surviving
  combat, food use, boss clears, or a separate XP rule to be designed.
- Add Mining, Smithing/Forging, Woodcutting, Archery, and Fletching to World 1 if scope allows. This
  better supports the OSRS-style economy loop and gives beta testers enough systems to live in
  Emberfall for 20+ hours.
- If implementation scope is tight, prioritize Archery + Mining + Smithing/Forging first, then
  Woodcutting + Fletching.

### World 1 Resource Tier Target

World 1 should introduce low-tier resource progression similar in spirit to old-school bronze/iron/
steel ladders without copying names directly if the final fantasy terminology changes.

Recommended beta target:

- Tier 1: crude/copper/bronze-equivalent.
- Tier 2: iron-equivalent.
- Tier 3: steel-equivalent.

World 1 crafted gear should cap around steel-equivalent power. The World 1 boss should drop items
clearly better than steel-equivalent gear. Higher iconic tiers should be held back; do not reach
rune-equivalent power by World 5. Around World 5, mithril-equivalent should be the upper neighborhood
at most.

### World 2-5 Skill Release Plan

World 2:

- Expand Mining/Smithing resource tiers.
- Expand Archery/Fletching materials.
- Add first World 2-specific resource node and forge recipe family.

World 3:

- Consider Crafting if item/equipment breadth needs it.
- Otherwise deepen existing gathering/crafting loops rather than adding a new skill too early.

World 4:

- Consider Enchanting if magic gear needs deeper identity.
- Otherwise hold new skills and focus on world mechanics.

World 5:

- First direct brush with hidden organization.
- Consider Alchemy/Potionmaking only if consumables need a full skill by this point.

### Long-Term Skill Brainstorm

Priority through World 5:

- Attack
- Strength
- Defence
- Health / HP
- Magic
- Archery
- Fishing
- Cooking
- Mining
- Smithing/Forging
- Woodcutting
- Fletching

Consider post-World-5:

- Crafting
- Enchanting
- Alchemy or Potionmaking
- Summoning/Taming
- Flight/Riding mastery, if travel becomes a major pillar

Explicitly deprioritized for now:

- Runecrafting.
- Agility.
- Sailing/Navigation.
- Farming.
- Hunting/Tracking.
- Engineering.
- Trading/Merchant as a skill. Player shops/markets can exist, but do not need a skill.
- Healing/Restoration as a skill.

## Ability Mastery Examples

### Meteor Strike

- Rank 1: one meteor, small radius, long cooldown.
- Rank 2: lower cooldown.
- Rank 3: larger radius.
- Rank 4: burning ground after impact.
- Rank 5: three meteors over two seconds.
- Rank 6: five meteors over three seconds, larger VFX and animation.

### Whirlwind

- Rank 1: short spin, small radius.
- Rank 2: lower cooldown.
- Rank 3: larger radius.
- Rank 4: extra hit pulse.
- Rank 5: longer duration.
- Rank 6: stronger animation, weapon trail, sparks, light pull.

### Blade Rush

- Rank 1: dash to target and stun.
- Rank 2: longer dash.
- Rank 3: longer stun.
- Rank 4: lower cooldown.
- Rank 5: impact cleave around target.
- Rank 6: cooldown refund on kill, upgraded dash trail.

## First Clear Rewards

### First Team Reward

The first team to clear Emberfall Dungeon receives:

- Unique title: `First Flame`.
- Permanent account badge: `Emberfall First Clear`.
- Cosmetic cape/banner/aura, no combat power.
- Team names engraved in Emberfall City near fountain or dungeon gate.
- Server-wide announcement.
- Optional statue or plaque in city.

Design rule:

- Reward must be prestigious, visible, and permanent.
- It should not create long-term combat imbalance.

### Server Reward

When the first team clears Emberfall:

- Eruption stabilizes.
- Emberfall Dungeon becomes easier for later clears.
- All players receive `Hope of Emberfall` while in World 1:
  - Suggested: +3% XP or +3% gold in Emberfall.
- City NPC dialogue updates.
- Dragon breach teaser becomes visible.

### Personal Clear Reward

Every player who personally clears World 1 receives:

- World 1 clear flag.
- Emberfang or Ember Focus choice.
- First necklace drop/roll:
  - Random necklace with minor stats or utility.
  - This introduces jewelry equipment without making it mandatory before the boss.
- 400 gold.
- Title later: `Emberfall Clearer`.
- Eligibility for World 2 once available.

## Equipment Slot Plan

Current equipment should expand to support:

- Weapon / main hand.
- Focus / off hand or magic implement.
- Helm.
- Chest.
- Legs.
- Cape.
- Gloves.
- Boots.
- Necklace.
- Ring 1.
- Ring 2.

World 1 should introduce the idea of jewelry through the first boss clear necklace roll. Rings can
exist as rare side drops or be held for World 2. Gloves and boots should become normal gear slots so
drop tables and crafting have more room to reward players without overloading weapon drops.
